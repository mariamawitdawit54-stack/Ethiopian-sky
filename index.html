From what we inherited to what we can discover.

03 · OUR PRINCIPLE

Curiosity needs evidence.

Ethiopian Sky does not treat an interesting historical claim as automatically true. We ask where the information comes from, what evidence supports it, what remains uncertain, and how different sources interpret it.

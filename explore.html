Explore the eight planets, then look beyond the Solar System at objects that can be seen from Ethiopian skies.

01 · SOLAR SYSTEM

The Eight Planets

Our Solar System has eight recognized planets. The first four are rocky terrestrial planets; Jupiter and Saturn are gas giants, while Uranus and Neptune are ice giants. Select a world to open its guide.

The five names shown below in Amharic are documented traditional planetary names: አጣርድ, ዝሁራ, መሪህ, መሽተሪ, ዙሐል. Uranus and Neptune are shown with modern Amharic transliterations only; this page does not assign traditional names to them.

02 · NIGHT-SKY OBJECTS

Look Beyond the Planets

Ethiopia's latitude gives observers access to a rich mixture of northern and southern sky objects. Start with bright naked-eye landmarks, then use binoculars or a telescope for deeper targets.

03 · OBSERVING IN ETHIOPIA

Make the Most of a Dark Sky

For beginners, the most important tools are a clear sky, a wide view of the horizon, and less artificial light. Let your eyes adapt to darkness, begin with bright targets, and use a planetarium app or star chart to confirm what you find.

Ethiopia also has dedicated astronomy facilities. The Space Science and Geospatial Institute's Entoto Observatory and Research Center offers guided astro-tourism and observing experiences, while its facilities include optical telescopes and sky-monitoring equipment.
